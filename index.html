About me

From San Miguel, El Salvador to Surabaya, Indonesia: one ocean, two homes, three languages.

I'm an industrial engineer with a master's degree in human factors and occupational safety from ITS in Surabaya. I study how people work with technology and how to make that work safer, and my thesis on safety support for artisanal divers became my first peer-reviewed article.

Teaching is the other half of my life. I have taught English to children, teenagers and university students across Indonesia, and I teach Spanish to Indonesian learners online.

Chapter 3 · 2024 – 2026 · Research

Going below the surface

At Kenjeran Beach, fishermen dive breathing air through a hose from a compressor on their boat. I set out to make that work safer.

2025 – 2026 · Master's thesis

A safety-support system for artisanal hookah divers

ITS and Kenjeran Beach, Surabaya · supervised by Retno Widyaningrum, Ph.D.

8fishermen I spoke with in the field
12 × 12requirements × engineering characteristics
4weighting scenarios, same ranking
2peer-reviewed publications
  • Observed the divers' work and turned field evidence, safety standards and dive-computer benchmarks into clear requirements.
  • Decided who gets which warning: a simple visual cue for the diver, and detailed status, a beeper and manual alarm for the attendant on the boat.
  • Used House of Quality, FMEA, decision matrices and traceability, and appraised the prototype built with the engineering firm Elmech.

What drives meTechnology should help people notice danger early and decide well, especially when an AI or a machine is giving advice.
